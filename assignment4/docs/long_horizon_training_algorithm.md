# Long-Horizon LoRA Experiment

## Objective

Measure how the selected step-150 adapter changes through a cumulative 500-step
horizon and test whether a lower-rate continuation can improve detail without
losing market structure, style, people, or prompt control.

The submitted adapter remains unchanged until a candidate wins a fixed-seed
visual comparison. Training loss is diagnostic only.

## Inputs

- `models/self_market_step150.safetensors`: selected step-150 adapter.
- `reproducibility/self_market_step150/training`: verified step-150 optimizer,
  scheduler, scaler, RNG, schedule, trace, and metrics state.
- 843 supplied images and fixed Florence-2 captions.
- 60 permitted images generated by unmodified SD 1.5 in `training_data`.
- Base model `runwayml/stable-diffusion-v1-5` and token `<sks>`.

No new image source is introduced. Assignment-PDF images, external datasets,
and adapter-generated training images remain forbidden.

## Trajectory A: Original Cosine Schedule

1. Copy the verified step-150 reproduction into
   `long_horizon_out/cosine_500` without modifying the source record.
2. Resume its existing optimizer, scheduler, scaler, RNG states, and fixed
   2,000-draw schedule.
3. Continue to optimizer step 500 with the original learning rates,
   preservation loss, token anchor, and cosine decay.
4. Save immutable adapters every 25 steps and resumable state at the same
   boundaries.
5. Stop for visual review at steps 250, 350, 450, and 500.

This trajectory isolates training duration. At step 200 its tensors must match
the earlier clean Candidate B step-200 checkpoint.

## Trajectory B: Stabilized Continuation

1. Initialize both LoRA branches and the learned token from the submitted
   step-150 adapter.
2. Start a fresh optimizer and independent deterministic schedule for 350 new
   steps, producing the same cumulative 500-step horizon.
3. Use the same selected training data with UNet LR `2e-5`, text-encoder LR
   `7.5e-7`, token LR `2.5e-6`, preservation weight `0.80`, token-anchor weight
   `0.12`, caption dropout `0.08`, and cosine decay with 25 warmup steps.
4. Save every 25 continuation steps. Review after continuation steps 100, 200,
   300, and 350, corresponding to cumulative steps 250, 350, 450, and 500.

The lower rates and stronger preservation terms test stability directly. Extra
self-generated images are deliberately excluded so duration and optimization
are not confounded with a data change.

## Visual Evaluation

For every 25 cumulative steps from 150 through 500, render at 512x512 with
DPM-Solver++, 30 inference steps, and guidance 7.5:

- exact assignment prompt, seed 84000;
- exact assignment prompt, seed 84003;
- close market-vendor portrait, seed 92003;
- two shoppers beside a vegetable stall, seed 92001.

Generate individual images, a manifest with hashes, and contact sheets covering
five checkpoints each. Inspect the rendered pixels for market readability,
stall geometry, crowd separation, faces, color stability, style fidelity, and
artifacts.

## Selection Gate

A long-horizon checkpoint may replace step 150 only if it:

1. improves at least three of the four trajectory-review columns without a
   severe regression in the fourth;
2. wins a blind ten-seed exact-prompt comparison against step 150;
3. retains populated markets on at least nine of ten seeds;
4. does not worsen the portrait and two-person controls overall; and
5. passes the adapter, token, data-provenance, and deterministic-inference
   checks required by the assignment.

If no checkpoint passes, step 150 remains final and the long-horizon outputs
are retained as an overtraining study.

## Expected Intermediate States

- Trajectory A has checkpoints 175 through 500 and 500 metric rows.
- Trajectory B has continuation checkpoints 25 through 350 and 350 metric rows.
- Each checkpoint has four deterministic review images.
- A session interruption resumes only from the last completed 25-step state.

## Edge Cases

- CUDA OOM: restart from the last immutable boundary after clearing VRAM.
- Loss decreases while images regress: retain the earlier checkpoint.
- Later images recover after an intermediate regression: compare the recovered
  checkpoint against step 150 on the blind ten-seed audit.
- Contract, schedule, or input hash mismatch: stop rather than bypassing the
  resume gate.
