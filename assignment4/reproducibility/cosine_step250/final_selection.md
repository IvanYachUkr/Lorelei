## Blind Holdouts

All rankings were written before the A/B/C mappings were read.

| Suite | Seeds | Submitted 150 wins | Cosine 250 wins | Stable 225 wins |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Exact assignment market prompt | 10 | 1 | 6 | 3 |
| Close smiling vendor | 5 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| Two shoppers at a vegetable stall | 5 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Total | 20 | 3 | 11 | 6 |

All three finalists produced populated markets on all ten assignment-prompt
seeds. Cosine step 250 won the target prompt most often, also won the close-face
control, and tied for first on the two-person control. It therefore passes the
replacement gate without trading market quality for portrait quality.

## Decision

Select original cosine cumulative step 250 as the final adapter.

The exact original trajectory converged after this point: steps 350-500 changed
pixels but did not improve scene organization, faces, or controls. The stabilized
continuation improved early crowd separation and peaked at cumulative step 225,
but lost the ten-seed market holdout 3-6 to cosine step 250.

The selected adapter was trained only from the 843 supplied style images and 60
fixed unmodified images generated by the base Stable Diffusion 1.5 model. No PDF
image, external dataset, or adapter-generated training image was used.
