# Selected Cosine Step 250

This directory contains the deterministic records and blind visual reviews for
the selected adapter. Its SHA-256 is
`fbcfb525c394f6c8f249196dae3c654a9dd08e9c232000e5bac95aba10805d8b`.

## Data

The run used all 843 supplied images and the 60 fixed PNGs in `training_data`.
Those auxiliary images were generated by unmodified Stable Diffusion 1.5. No
assignment-PDF image, external dataset, or adapter-generated image was used.
Florence-2 captions are fixed metadata and Florence is not loaded during
training or evaluation.

## Reproduction

Run from the `assignment4` directory:

```bat
python code\train_lora.py ^
  --data_dir style_imgs\512 ^
  --captions_jsonl code\auto_captions\florence_captions.jsonl ^
  --auxiliary_jsonl training_data\auxiliary.jsonl ^
  --instance_token "<sks>" ^
  --token_initializer "ghibli style" ^
  --output_dir reproducibility\cosine_step250\reproduced_model ^
  --provenance_dir reproducibility\cosine_step250\reproduced_training ^
  --rank 16 ^
  --text_encoder_rank 4 ^
  --learning_rate 7e-5 ^
  --text_encoder_learning_rate 2.5e-6 ^
  --token_learning_rate 1e-5 ^
  --lora_dropout 0.05 ^
  --caption_dropout_prob 0.08 ^
  --snr_gamma 5.0 ^
  --preservation_loss_weight 0.65 ^
  --token_anchor_loss_weight 0.05 ^
  --lr_scheduler cosine ^
  --lr_warmup_steps 50 ^
  --max_steps 500 ^
  --stop_after_step 250 ^
  --checkpointing_steps 25 ^
  --gradient_accumulation_steps 4 ^
  --gradient_checkpointing ^
  --random_flip ^
  --allow_tf32 ^
  --seed 2202 ^
  --overwrite
```

`max_steps=500` is intentional: it fixes the cosine trajectory and complete
2,000-draw schedule. `stop_after_step=250` selects the reviewed checkpoint.
The committed schedule contains all 2,000 planned draws. Rows 1-1,000 of the
trace and 1-250 of the metrics produce the selected model; later rows document
the convergence experiment through step 500.

The original run first stopped at step 150 and then resumed from its complete
optimizer, scheduler, scaler, token, and RNG state. `run_config.json` records
that immutable first session; `sessions.jsonl` records every continuation. Exact
state equivalence was checked at cumulative steps 175 and 200 for all 353 saved
tensors and every non-timing log field. The result is in
`continuation_verification.json`.

## Verification

After the reproduction finishes:

```bat
python code\verify_reproduced_model.py ^
  --selected lora_out\pytorch_lora_weights.safetensors ^
  --reproduced reproducibility\cosine_step250\reproduced_model\pytorch_lora_weights.safetensors ^
  --training_dir reproducibility\cosine_step250\reproduced_training ^
  --reference_training_dir reproducibility\cosine_step250\training ^
  --data_registry training_data\registry.json ^
  --selected_step 250 ^
  --out reproducibility\cosine_step250\reproduction_verification.json
```

## Selection

The finalist mappings were hidden until each ranking was written. Across ten
assignment-prompt seeds, five close-vendor seeds, and five two-shopper seeds,
cosine step 250 won 11 comparisons, stable cumulative step 225 won 6, and the
submitted step 150 model won 3. All three produced populated markets for all ten
assignment-prompt seeds. The rankings, generation manifests, and mappings are
under `selection`; the reviewed grids are under `samples/comparisons`.
